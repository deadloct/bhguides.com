Sora's Cursed Calavera Grunt Clear

This guide was compiled from Sora's messages in the Official Discord server with edits by BillyIdol.

Build

Perkunas with different configurations for mobs and Grunturgh.

Against Mobs

  • Equipment: Full Perkunas
  • Accessory: Defensive
  • Full block enchants and runes (89%)
  • Redirect rune
  • Note: defensive accessory here, unlike the offensive accessory used against Grunturgh

Against Grunturgh

  • Equipment: 3/4 Perkunas with Cursed Calavera mainhand from T10 Netherworld WB
  • Accessory: Offensive
  • Enchantments: dual strike (15.8%)
  • Major Runes: empower (13.35%)
  • Meta Rune: Yuh — 20% chance for first attack to be empowered
  • Down Triangle Rune: Othale — while at full HP, gain 25% empower

Familiars

Ulairius x2

  • Pump: deflect
  • Chip: evade
  • Bone: redirect
  • Brain: heal and shield team when hit

Remmyt

  • Pump: dual strike
  • Chip: heal
  • Bone: increased healing received
  • Brain: spread heal

Strategy

Team composition from front to back: Ulairius/Ulairius/Remmyt/Sora

Sora used 3/4 Perkunas with the Cursed Calavera mainhand (from T10 Netherworld WB). The team lined up with 2 Ulairius upfront, a Remmyt, and Sora in the last position. Against the boss, Sora used its 2sp skill to one-shot Grunturgh. The empower runes, specifically the meta rune, increased the damage of that attack and allowed for the one-shot kill.

Don't use the cursed mainhand before the Grunturgh fight. Right before the fight, swap to the cursed mainhand, switch to the empower runes/enchants, and equip the offensive accessory.
